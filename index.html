How We Work

Discover – Understand – Plan – Develop – Deliver. Our client-focused process takes a solution from the first conversation through launch and ongoing improvement.

  1. Discovery & consultationDiscuss your business, goals, challenges, and expectations.
  2. Requirements & analysisDefine the problem and the functional and technical requirements.
  3. Planning & proposalAgree scope, solution, technology, timeline, deliverables, and budget.
  4. Design & developmentBuild and integrate the solution with regular feedback and collaboration.
  5. Testing & quality assuranceCheck usability, reliability, security, functionality, and compatibility.
  6. Delivery & deploymentLaunch or hand over the solution with agreed documentation and training.
  7. Support & continuous improvementProvide agreed maintenance, troubleshooting, updates, and enhancements.
