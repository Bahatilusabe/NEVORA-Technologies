Built around your operation

A clearer view of the work behind every order.

Manufacturing teams rely on many connected activities: purchasing the right materials, planning production, tracking stock and fulfilling customer orders. When information is scattered across manual processes, it becomes harder to see what is ready, what needs attention and where delays may arise.

NEVORA works with your team to understand how your business runs and shape an ERP solution around its priorities. The system scope is defined with you, so the workflows and reporting fit your operation rather than forcing your people into a one-size-fits-all process.

What we can plan together

Connect the workflows that keep production moving.

Capabilities are scoped to your business processes, products and operating model. A manufacturing ERP implementation may bring together areas such as:

01

Production planning

Coordinate jobs, schedules and operational priorities with a clearer view of planned work.

02

Materials and inventory

Connect purchasing and stock processes to help teams understand material availability and movement.

03

Orders and fulfilment

Improve coordination between customer orders, production activity and delivery workflows.

04

Cost and operational visibility

Bring relevant operational information together to support better-informed business decisions.

05

Quality and process checks

Map the checks and approvals that matter to your production process into agreed workflows.

06

Business-wide reporting

Give teams and decision-makers a more connected view of the measures they need to monitor.

From discovery to ongoing improvement

A practical path to implementation.

  1. Understand your processesWe learn how your teams work today and identify the priorities for the system.
  2. Plan and configureWe agree the workflows, requirements and rollout approach before configuring the solution.
  3. Test, train and refineWe support validation and team readiness, then identify improvements after go-live.
